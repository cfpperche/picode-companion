01 / THE IDEA

From your browser window
to a place on your desk.

PiCode brings your agents, projects and decisions together in a development environment. Companion is designed to give that work a physical presence.

The idea is simple: have a conversation, show an object and notice when an agent needs you. All while staying connected to the right project and session.

Microphones, a speaker and audio processing to talk with the agent and answer its questions.

Show what

The autofocus camera is the candidate for viewing objects, circuit boards and documents on your desk.

03 / PRESENCE

Your agents,
closer to you.

Expressions, notifications and physical controls to follow the work and step in when a decision is needed.
